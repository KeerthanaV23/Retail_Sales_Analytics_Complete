# Business Insights

## Objective
Analyze retail sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance, customer activity and regional performance.

## Key questions
- Which category generates the most revenue?
- Which region performs best?
- Which products are top sellers?
- How does revenue change month by month?
- How many customers place repeat orders?

## Recommended business actions
1. Focus inventory and promotions on high-performing products.
2. Investigate lower-performing regions for improvement opportunities.
3. Monitor monthly revenue trends to identify growth or decline.
4. Identify repeat customers and develop retention strategies.
